About Me

I'm a developer who blends hands-on engineering with the habit of explaining it clearly — five years spent shipping production web software and just as long teaching ICT students how it all fits together.

My work follows the full lifecycle: planning, designing, building, testing, and deploying software that ranges from marketing websites to internal tools and small mobile apps. I care as much about maintainable code as I do about the person who inherits it after me.

Outside of client work, I lecture part-time on web systems and software engineering fundamentals — which keeps me honest about writing code other people can actually read.
